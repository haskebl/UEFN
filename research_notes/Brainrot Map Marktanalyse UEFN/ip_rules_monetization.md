## 1. IP: Who owns/claims the Italian Brainrot characters, and is it safe to use them in a Fortnite island?

### Takeaway
The characters are **not free-to-use memes any more**. Two of them, **Tung Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina, are now licensed Fortnite Item Shop IP**: Mementum Lab and Epic launched official skins on 2026-04-01. Tung Tung Tung Sahur is also at the centre of a live US federal lawsuit (Spyder Games v. Mementum Lab, N.D. Cal. 5:25-cv-10248), and US trademark applications are pending for "TRALALERO TRALALA" (Notice of Allowance issued) and "TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHUR" (published for opposition). Using these names or likenesses in a monetized island without a license is **HIGH risk** for Tung Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina and **MEDIUM-HIGH** for Tralalero Tralala. For the other characters the risk is **MEDIUM (unknown claimants)**. The safest route is original "brainrot-style" characters with new names.

### Inferences
- **Impact:** Epic now has a commercial relationship with Mementum and sells the characters itself. That makes it very likely Epic will **honor IP complaints from Mementum** against unlicensed Tung Tung Tung Sahur or Ballerina Cappuccina islands (removal or delisting and loss of payouts), regardless of how the court case ends. The lawsuit's copyright outcome does not remove the trademark angle.
- **Solution (ranked):**
  1. Create **original brainrot-style characters** with new, non-confusable names, for example an original fruit-animal-vehicle hybrid with a rhyming pseudo-Italian name. The genre, style and "Italian TTS chant" format are not ownable. Specific names and designs are what is claimed.
  2. If you want the famous characters, **apply to join Mementum's Fortnite partner program** (the Discover-row partners) for a license.
  3. Do not use the protected names in titles, tags or thumbnails. Metadata keyword-stuffing with "Tung Tung Sahur" is the highest-exposure use.
- **Risk by character** (for a monetized public island, Sept 2026):
  - Tung Tung Tung Sahur: **HIGH** (active litigation, Mementum claim, pending trademark, sold by Epic)
  - Ballerina Cappuccina: **HIGH** (Mementum claim, sold by Epic)
  - Tralalero Tralala: **MEDIUM-HIGH** (class 9 US trademark allowed to ASI Games)

## 2. Epic rules for publishing: IP, metadata, thumbnails, XP farms, AI disclosure, IARC, children, gambling

### Takeaway
The Fortnite Developer Rules (formerly "Island Creator Rules", at legal.epicgames.com) prohibit the following:
- misleading metadata, and V-Bucks or real-money imagery in thumbnails
- "XP", "AFK" and "coin farm" terms in metadata
- targeting under-13s as the primary audience
- duplicate islands (new Rule 1.9.1, 2026-03-05)
- **selling anything that influences prize wheels** (from 2026-01-20)

Paid random items are allowed only with disclosed odds and are restricted by region. I found no island-level AI-content disclosure requirement in UEFN or the Creator Portal as of 2026 (UNVERIFIED absence).

### Cited Findings
- The rules live at [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules), with a [Change Log](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules-change-log). [VERIFIED official]
- **Rule 1.9.1 (added 2026-03-05):** you may not publish multiple copies of the same island or metadata to manipulate visibility or engagement. [VERIFIED official change log, via snippet] — [Change Log](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules-change-log)
- **Metadata:** thumbnails, descriptions and titles must accurately represent the island, and you may not mislead players anywhere in it. [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-island-creator-rules?lang=en-US)
- **Thumbnails:** you must not mention V-Bucks, the Battle Pass, real-world currency or rewards, and must not include images of V-Bucks or real currency. [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [Thumbnail Image Policies](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/thumbnail-image-policies?lang=en-US)
- **XP / engagement bait:** you may not promise or mislead about XP, items or Quests. The terms "AFK", "XP", "Coin farm" and "Coin slide" are banned in the name, description, thumbnail, loading screen and lobby background. Clickbait and abuse of Support-A-Creator are also prohibited. [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules); background: [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/fortnite/island-changes)
- **Epic IP:** you may not recreate Epic IP that is not provided in UEFN, such as Battle Royale season assets. Islands using IP Program Assets must not include other third-party IP. [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-island-creator-rules?lang=en-US)
- **UEFN assets:** Epic-owned assets supplied in UEFN may be used, including in items for sale. Assets, characters or music recreated or obtained from Fortnite or other Epic properties may not. [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [UEFN Supplemental Terms](https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/uefn)
- **Children:** Epic does not accept content whose primary audience is under-13s, and you must not target under-13s with advertising, promotion or communication. [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules)
- **IARC:** an IARC rating is required on publishing. The questionnaire must accurately cover all content, including visuals, dialog, themes and **music with lyrics**. [VERIFIED official] — [IARC Overview and FAQs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/iarc-overview-and-faqs-in-fortnite-creative?lang=en-US). Islands with IIT **must declare "Digital Purchases"** in the IARC questionnaire. [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [IIT Overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/in-island-transactions-overview-in-fortnite?lang=en-US)
- **Prize wheels:** a new rule bans in-island transactions that "directly or indirectly influence prize wheels", effective **2026-01-20**. That covers selling spins, extra spins, bundles and luck boosts. It followed backlash over *Steal the Brainrot* (Fortnite). [LIKELY, multiple outlets] — [Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/epic-games-bans-prize-wheel-and-loot-box-sales-in-fortnite-creator-islands/); [BigGo 2026-01-17](https://biggo.com/news/202601170022_Fortnite-bans-paid-prize-wheels-in-creator-islands); [UE forum announcement](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/update-to-fortnite-developer-rules-in-island-transactions-and-prize-wheels/2692650)
- **Paid random items (Rule 4.4.9, updated 2026-01-10):**
  - You must disclose the actual numerical odds before purchase, use non-misleading and non-pressuring language, and use the PaidRandomItem field.
  - Restricted in Singapore, Qatar, Australia, Netherlands, Belgium and Brazil, and in the UK for players under 18.
  - [LIKELY/official via snippet] — [Change Log](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules-change-log); [IIT Restrictions](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/in-island-transactions-restrictions-in-fortnite); [Gaming Amigos](https://www.gamingamigos.com/post/rules-paid-random-items-fortnite)
- **Other IIT rules:**
  - 4.4.12: disclose items that give a meaningful gameplay advantage.
  - 4.4.13: disclose offers that give access to paid areas.
  - 4.4.14: no transactions that give nothing in exchange for V-Bucks.
  - 4.4.7 (updated 2026-04-29): offers that visually overlap Item Shop items must describe their gameplay value and set ConsequentialToGameplay = true.
  - In some regions you cannot directly prompt players under 18 to buy.
  - [VERIFIED official change log / docs, via snippet] — [Change Log](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules-change-log); [IIT Restrictions](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/in-island-transactions-restrictions-in-fortnite)
- **No Outfits or Emotes:** in-island transactions may not grant Outfits or Emotes, however they are described. [LIKELY] — [Tubefilter 2026-01-09](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/09/fortnites-new-developer-made-item-sales-loot-boxes-yes-outfits-and-emotes-no/)
- **AI disclosure:**
  - I found no Fortnite or UEFN island-level AI-content disclosure requirement.
  - Tim Sweeney publicly dismissed store-level AI disclosure in late 2025 ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/epic-games-ceo-says-its-really-irresponsible-of-steam-to-make-studios-disclose-ai-use/)).
  - Epic's **Fab** marketplace requires a mandatory "Created with AI" flag for AI-generated listings ([Promise Legal blog](https://blog.promise.legal/ai-game-assets-what-studios-own-2026/)). [UNVERIFIED as to absence of an island requirement]

### Inferences
- **Impact for a brainrot map:**
  - Titles like "Steal the Brainrot – FREE XP / AFK" break the XP/AFK rule.
  - Thumbnails with V-Bucks piles break the thumbnail policy.
  - Duplicate "v2" islands break Rule 1.9.1.
  - A brainrot "spin the wheel" mechanic must **not** be sold. Earned-only free wheels appear allowed. That is an inference: the ban targets transactions influencing wheels.
  - Brainrot content skews young, but Epic forbids targeting under-13s. Metadata and marketing must not address "kids".
- **Solution:**
  - Use clean titles without XP, AFK or V-Bucks.
  - Make the thumbnail show actual gameplay with original characters.
  - Answer the IARC questionnaire honestly: cartoon violence (bat hits), crude humor, Digital Purchases, and user interaction if voice or text is on.
  - Avoid paid random items entirely, or disclose odds and region-gate them.
  - Keep one canonical island.
- **Risk:** MEDIUM if the rules are ignored (delisting, loss of payouts, account strikes). LOW if followed.
- **Validation:** before publishing, read the full current rule text of sections 1.x (metadata), 3.x (content) and 4.4.x (IIT) at legal.epicgames.com, plus the change log after 2026-04-29.

### Gaps
- I could not read the full text of the Developer Rules, so section numbers other than 1.9.1 and 4.4.7/9/12/13/14 are not confirmed.
- No confirmation either way of a 2026 AI-disclosure field in the Creator Portal.
- The exact IARC content triggers (for example, what counts for "Everyone 10+" versus "Teen") come from general IARC practice, not Epic-specific text.

## 3. Monetization status, Sept 2026: Engagement Payouts, IIT, Sponsored Row, eligibility

### Takeaway
- **Engagement payouts** remain funded by **40% of eligible Fortnite net revenue**, distributed by Epic's (unpublished) engagement formula.
- **In-island transactions (IIT)** went live on **2026-01-09**. Creators get **100% of V-Bucks value (about 74% of the purchase price) until 2027-01-31**, then **50% from 2027-02-01**.
- IIT earnings are paid separately and do not reduce engagement payouts.
- To monetize, you must be 18+ and enrolled in the Fortnite Developer Program.

### Cited Findings
- **Engagement pool:** 40% of eligible net revenue from the Item Shop and related real-money purchases goes into the Engagement Pool, which is distributed across Epic's and creators' islands by the Engagement Payout Formula. [VERIFIED official] — [Engagement Payout docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/engagement-payout-in-fortnite-creative?lang=en-US)
- **Formula inputs** (as reported for 2026):
  - active playtime
  - retention (players returning on later days)
  - **playtime in the week around a player's Item Shop spend** (added with the late-2025 update)
  - Epic does not publish exact weights. The older 2023 components (engagement, new-player retention, lapsed-player retention) were superseded or merged.
  - [LIKELY; component list from secondary sources] — [Fortnite news: developers will soon be able to sell in-game items](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-developers-will-soon-be-able-to-sell-in-game-items); [gemlist.io](https://www.gemlist.io/blog/fortnite-uefn-creator-monetization); [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/articles/fortnite-introduces-marketplace-ads-engagement-141249469.html)
- **IIT launch:** publishing IIT was delayed from late 2025 and fully rolled out **2026-01-09**. [LIKELY] — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/epic-games-rolls-out-in-island-transactions-for-fortnite-creative/); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/fortnite-now-lets-creators-publish-islands-with-in-game-transactions/1100-6537294/)
- **IIT revenue share:**
  - 100% of V-Bucks value through **2027-01-31**, then **50% from 2027-02-01**. "V-Bucks value" is net of platform and store fees, so 100% works out to about 74% of the money spent.
  - IIT revenue is paid alongside the monthly engagement payout and **does not affect engagement payouts**.
  - [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [Tools for IIT now in UEFN](https://www.fortnite.com/news/tools-for-in-island-transactions-now-available-to-fortnite-developers?lang=en-US)
  - **Conflict:** earlier coverage said "100% through 2026", and Tubefilter says the split begins "in 2028". Treat the official 2027-01-31 / 2027-02-01 dates as authoritative. — [Tubefilter 2025-09-18](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/09/18/fortnite-challenges-roblox-with-in-game-item-sales-zero-revenue-split-through-2027/); [Tubefilter 2026-01-09](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/09/fortnites-new-developer-made-item-sales-loot-boxes-yes-outfits-and-emotes-no/)
- **IIT eligibility:**
  - Owner at least 18 years old and enrolled in the Fortnite Developer Program.
  - Program entry requires age 18+ and either a qualifying Epic Payments purchase or at least $20 spent in Fortnite in the last 365 days.
  - Tax and payment info is required through program enrollment (standard; not separately confirmed).
  - [VERIFIED official, via snippet] — [IIT Overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/in-island-transactions-overview-in-fortnite?lang=en-US); [Developer Program eligibility change](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-island-creator-program-eligibility-change); [Enroll](https://create.fortnite.com/enroll)
- **Item types:**
  - Allowed: durable and consumable items, custom passes, progression systems, paid areas, and paid random items (with odds disclosed and regional limits).
  - Not allowed: Outfits or Emotes, anything that influences a prize wheel, and anything that gives nothing in return.
  - [LIKELY/official via snippet] — [IIT Overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/in-island-transactions-overview-in-fortnite?lang=en-US); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/09/fortnites-new-developer-made-item-sales-loot-boxes-yes-outfits-and-emotes-no/)
- **Pay-to-win:** allowed but must be disclosed. Items giving a meaningful gameplay advantage must be disclosed under Rule 4.4.12, and ConsequentialToGameplay must be flagged under Rule 4.4.7. [VERIFIED official change log, via snippet] — [Change Log](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules-change-log)
- **Sponsored Row:**
  - Launched November 2025. It is a paid auction for placement in Discover.
  - Eligible: Developer Program members aged 18+, with a published, listed island in good standing.
  - Through the end of 2026, 100% of Sponsored Row revenue goes into the engagement pool. Long-term it will be 50%.
  - [VERIFIED official] — [Sponsored Row campaign tools](https://www.fortnite.com/news/sponsored-row-campaign-tools-now-in-the-fortnite-creator-portal); [Campaign FAQs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/campaign-faqs-in-fortnite); [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/fortnite-will-soon-let-island-creators-sell-in-game-items-and-pay-to-appear-in-a-new-sponsored-row/)
- **Scale:**
  - Epic has paid more than $1B to UEFN creators since launch.
  - More than 3,000 islands earn more from IIT than from engagement payouts.
