## 3. Loop timing, progression, monetization and retention

### Takeaway
The benchmark retention stack in Sept 2026, verified mainly for Steal the Brainrot:
- 6-digit promo codes, pushed via Discord, X and TikTok
- Rebirth with a lengthening lock timer
- Hourly or weekend "Admin Abuse" events with luck multipliers
- A crafting machine
- Event-themed limited Brainrots
- Since Jan 2026, V-Bucks in-island transactions

Times to first reward, upgrade and rare were **not found** in any text source.

- **Codes as a growth channel.** Steal the Brainrot code rewards (Sept 2026) include:
  - 4x Cash Multi
  - Eternal Machine Cooldown Skip
  - Machine Luck Boost
  - Luck Level Boost
  - Spawns of specific Brainrots (Ferinsini Luckini, Toxic Llama Rot, Lovely Llama Rot)
  - Jungle Wave + Cupidbeasts

## 4. Weaknesses and player complaints to exploit

### Takeaway
The best-documented weakness is **predatory, gambling-like monetization aimed at kids**. It drew mainstream press backlash and a platform rule change. Complaints about fairness in admin-driven events also exist. I could not reach Reddit, YouTube comments, TikTok or fortnite.gg reviews, so gameplay-level complaints (grind, lag, base-steal griefing) are **unverified**.

### Cited Findings
- Players called the wheel and bundles "predatory gambling" aimed at kids and compared them to Epic's earlier loot-box lawsuits — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-gambling-style-mechanics/); [GamingBible ("Disgraceful")](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/fortnite-gambling-feature-outrage-483800-20260112); [Beebom](https://beebom.com/fortnite-players-push-back-against-steal-the-brainrots-gambling-style-v-bucks-system/)

- Players criticized the fake "discount" framing above the platform's price cap — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-gambling-style-mechanics/)
- Complaints about Admin Abuse events: alleged favoritism, selective bans and inconsistent enforcement. This may refer to the Roblox original — [fortniteindex](https://fortniteindex.com/posts/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-admin-abuse-today)
- Stealing takes a very long time at high rebirths. The lock grows to 250s, and Secret-tier rebirth requirements are "close to a lottery", which points to a late-game grind wall — [respawn-daily](https://respawn-daily.com/en/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-rebirth-guide-requirements/) (CLAIMED)
- Platform level: an "influx of spam maps" in Discovery led Epic to tighten review. This matters for clones — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/fortnite/epic-games-speed-review-process-fortnite-creative-maps?ref=homepage)
- IP risk: the Roblox Steal a Brainrot developers sued a Fortnite copycat ("Stealing Brainrots") — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/roblox-steal-a-brainrot-dev-sues-fortnite-copycat/); [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/brainrot-roblox-court/). There is also a debate over who owns AI-generated brainrot IP — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/who-owns-brainrot-fortnite-skin-launch-renews-creator-community-debate-over-ai-generated-ip/)

### Inferences
- Positioning to exploit:
  1. "Fair" monetization: no paid RNG, visible odds, cosmetics-first.
  2. Less punishing steal/PvP, or a PvE hybrid like Fight or Garden, since those retain very well.
  3. Original characters instead of copied Roblox IP.
- Steal-style PvP griefing complaints are very likely, but I did not verify them. Luis should mine the comments manually (Section 6, R-C).
