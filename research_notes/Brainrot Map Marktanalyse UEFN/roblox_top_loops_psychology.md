## Q1: Which loops drove the most CCU and revenue on Roblox (2025–2026), and what is rising in summer/autumn 2026?

### Takeaway
2025's all-time CCU records all came from **idle-collection "steal/grow" loops**: Grow a Garden (~21–22M), Steal a Brainrot (~25M), 99 Nights (14.2M) and Plants vs Brainrots (~8.7M). Revenue in 2026, however, is led by long-running games (Rivals, Blox Fruits, Brookhaven, Adopt Me, PS99). The breakout of autumn 2026 is **Steal An Egg**: a Steal-a-Brainrot-style steal loop with pets and eggs, launched on 2026-07-25, which peaked at about 14M CCU on 2026-09-18/19. The steal-and-collect loop is still the strongest viral format as of Sep 2026.

### Inferences
- The same pattern shows up four times at 8M+ CCU (GaG, SaB, PvB, Steal An Egg): **idle $/s collectibles in a personal base, a rarity ladder, shared-server social exposure, and weekly timed events**. It keeps producing hits 12+ months later, so it is not a one-off fad.
- Revenue estimates for SaB (~$3–7M/month) are low next to its CCU. Brainrot audiences are young, mobile-heavy and low-ARPU (RoWatcher notes "mobile-dominant"). CCU virality ≠ revenue leadership.
- Sequels and clones decay fast: GaG2 peaked under 1M. Novelty in the collectible theme (eggs/pets vs brainrots) matters more than the brand.

## Q2: Deconstruction of the 5 most relevant brainrot-loop games (30s / 5min / 1h / D2 / W2) and the levers at each stage

Selected games: **Steal a Brainrot, Grow a Garden, Plants vs Brainrots, Steal An Egg** (strongest 2026 riser, same loop) and **99 Nights in the Forest** (biggest non-idle 2025 hit and the only one with a film deal). Timing numbers come from guides or wikis (CLAIMED by the community, not developer-published).

### Takeaway
Every stage is fast, cheap and visible:
- **First 30s**: you are handed a base, and the first collectible or seed is affordable right away.
- **First 5 min**: the first income tick, a first shop restock, or (in SaB) the first theft.
- **First hour**: the first rare, plus exposure to other players' flexing.
- **Day 2**: offline progress pays out.
- **Week 2**: weekly Saturday updates, admin-abuse events and trading/status carry retention.
The loss-aversion lever (base theft with a lock timer) is what separates SaB/Steal An Egg from GaG.

### Cited Findings — mechanics with timing
**Steal a Brainrot**
- On joining a server, your base is **locked for 30s**. After that you can re-lock it for **60s**, and each rebirth adds +10s of lock — [Roblox fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/BRAZILIAN_SPYDER/Steal_a_Brainrot), [games.gg guide](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/steal-a-brainrot-ultimate-beginners-guide/).
- Brainrots in your base generate **cash per second**. Income scales exponentially with rarity and mutations/traits — [bloxspot wiki](https://bloxspot.com/steal-a-brainrot-wiki-2026-complete-guide-brainrot-list-rebirths-gears-stealing-tips/).
- **First rebirth** requires $1M plus two specific brainrots. It resets cash, brainrots and base in exchange for +1 money multiplier, +10s lock and stronger shop items — [SaB wiki Rebirth](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth), [Sportskeeda Rebirth 10](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-brainrot-rebirth-10-guide).
- The **Secret** rarity tier reaches $2.5M/s to $100M/s per unit, an extreme power-law ladder — [bloxspot](https://bloxspot.com/steal-a-brainrot-wiki-2026-complete-guide-brainrot-list-rebirths-gears-stealing-tips/).
- **Admin Abuse** events are hosted by the developer SpyderSammy: he joins servers, spawns items and causes chaos, mostly on schedule — [eldorado.gg](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/sammy-steal-a-brainrot-guide/), [SaB fandom](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/SpyderSammy).
- The **Aug 23, 2025 admin-abuse "war"** vs Grow a Garden ended with GaG at 22.3M and SaB at 20.3M. The event was co-promoted by KreekCraft, MrBeast and others — [eldorado.gg](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/sammy-steal-a-brainrot-guide/).
- A post-update admin abuse pushed the peak to 24.9M (TikTok, CLAIMED) — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@thebartblox/video/7554938511426719031).
- One analysis frames the core as **capture-the-flag**: take brainrots from others and return them to your base, or buy them from the conveyor/market — [Naavik snippet](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/).

**Grow a Garden**
- A carrot grows in **1–2 min** and costs 10 Sheckles, so the first harvest-and-sell happens inside the first few minutes — [mygagcalculator](https://mygagcalculator.com/grow-a-garden-farming-guide/).
- **Crops grow while offline.** Weather mutations (Shocked, Celestial, Aurora…) happen **only while online**. Night raises mutation chance — [droidgamers](https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/can-crops-get-mutations-when-youre-offline-in-grow-a-garden/), [GameSpot weather](https://www.gamespot.com/gallery/grow-a-garden-weather-events/2900-7058/).
- **Shop RNG restocks**: seeds and gear every 5 min, eggs every 30 min, Travelling Merchant every 2 h. Each seed restock is a fresh RNG draw — [gagcalculatorvalue stock](https://gagcalculatorvalue.com/stock).
- Weekly updates go live **Saturday 7am PST**, with celebratory gifts for players online at launch. The game grew from 500 to ~100k CCU in two weeks after Jandel/Splitting Point joined and started a live-ops cadence — [GamesBeat interview with Janzen Madsen](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/).
- GameDiscoverCo attributes the success to simple gameplay plus "its ability to reward users passively" and time-gated weekly exclusives — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu).

**Plants vs Brainrots**
- Loop: buy seeds → plant → plants auto-fight brainrots → defeated brainrots drop money → buy stronger plants. Plants level up to max level 5 from kills — [techwiser](https://techwiser.com/plants-vs-brainrots-all-plants-brainrots-list/), [plantsbrainrots.net](https://plantsbrainrots.net/).
- A **Brainrot Invasion** unlocks every **20–30 active minutes**. Losing a wheelbarrow fails the mission. Plants have HP and temporarily disappear — [Sportskeeda storyline](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/plants-vs-brainrots-storyline-system-guide), [Beebom story mode](https://beebom.com/plants-vs-brainrots-story-mode-explained/).
- Codes are heavily used for early boosts — [GamesRadar codes](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/plants-vs-brainrots-codes/).

**Steal An Egg**
- Loop: grab an egg (press E) from a nest guarded by beasts → run back to base → hatch it (rare eggs take **hours**; size and mutation change hatch time) → the pet generates income → upgrade treadmill/speed for harder biomes — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-an-egg-a-beginner-s-guide), [Steal An Egg wiki](https://stealanegg.fandom.com/wiki/Eggs).
- The treadmill had an embedded short-video feed (AI clips, likes, comments). It was removed after the takedown, and Roblox said it will remove experiences that reward watching a continuous content feed as inappropriate for younger users — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/roblox-takes-down-controversial-doomscrolling-game-promises-to-remove-others-that-reward-watching-a-continuous-feed-of-content-as-theyre-not-appropriate-for-younger-users/).

**99 Nights in the Forest**
- Co-op survival with a visible night counter as the progression goal. Peak was 14.2M, and the IP has moved into film and a Fortnite crossover — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/with-a-peak-player-count-of-14-2-million-99-nights-in-the-forest-has-an-audience-other-multiplayer-games-would-kill-for-to-find-these-behemoth-playerbases-you-need-to-be-on-a-platform-like-roblox/). No detailed timing data was found.

### Phase × lever matrix (synthesis; the mechanics are cited above, the placement in each phase is INFERENCE)
| Phase | SaB / Steal An Egg | Grow a Garden | Plants vs Brainrots | Levers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–30s | Base assigned. 30s join-lock protects the newbie. A cheap brainrot is affordable on the conveyor (or an egg is nearby) | Plot assigned; buy a carrot for 10 | Buy seed, plant it | Ownership, instant affordance, comic meme characters (curiosity) |
| ~5 min | First $/s ticks. First sight of someone stealing, or first own steal attempt. Lock timer tension | First harvest-and-sell (carrot 1–2 min); first 5-min shop restock | First kills give cash; plant levels up | Variable reward (restock RNG), loss aversion, theft/revenge |
| ~1 h | First Mythic/Secret seen in another base; rebirth goal visible ($1M) | First mutation during a weather event; eggs (30-min restock) give pets | First Invasion (20–30 active min) | Status/rarity, social comparison, collection drive, timed events |
| Day 2 | Offline income (SaB) or hatch timers of hours (Egg) pull players back. Codes | Offline growth pays out; online-only mutations reward being there live | Codes, return for plant upgrades | Offline progress, appointment mechanics |
| Week 2 | Rebirth loop (reset for multiplier); admin abuse; new brainrots each update | Saturday update plus gifts for attending; limited items; trading economy | Updates, story missions | Timed events and FOMO, admin-abuse spectacle, trading/status, creator/YouTuber hype |

### Inferences
- **Loss aversion + revenge (theft) is the SaB/Steal An Egg differentiator** and fuels the social and streaming spectacle. Lock timers (30s/60s, +10s per rebirth) are a tuned fairness valve.
- **Offline progress + online-only bonuses** (GaG) is an elegant D1 hook: progress while away, extra reward for returning during events.
- **Scheduled developer-hosted events** (admin abuse, Saturday drops) create synchronized CCU spikes that break records and feed the algorithm. Every record peak above was tied to such an event.

- No official time-to-first-rare, session length or D1/D7 for any of these five games found. Timings above come from community guides.
- No YouTube "first hour" transcripts were accessible (fetch blocked).
- No confirmed developer postmortems (DevForum/RDC) for SaB or PvB found. The GamesBeat Jandel interview is the only primary developer source.

## Q3: Timing and retention benchmarks (D1/D7, session length) for Roblox simulators

### Takeaway
No published D1/D7 for top brainrot games was found. DevForum anecdotes from small simulators show D1 of about 2.5–10% and sessions of 5–15 min. Community targets are about 8 min average playtime and ≥10% D1.

### Cited Findings
- Community target: aim for ≥8 min playtime and ≥10% D1 (CLAIMED, DevForum user) — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-fix-very-low-d1-retention/2514260).
- Small-simulator cases (CLAIMED/self-reported):
  - 10–15 min average visit, D1 falling from 10% to 3%.
  - 5 min playtime with 2.5% D1.
  - ~7% D1.
  - Sources: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/high-session-length-but-low-retention-rate/1752184), [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/suggestions-to-improve-average-session-time-day-1-retention/2935525), [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/help-getting-d1-and-d7-retention-up/3159449).
- Roblox Analytics defines D1/D7 and the related metrics — [Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started).
- GaG in May 2025: 1.2B hours played (CLAIMED/press). Divided by visits this implies long sessions, but no per-session figure was published.

### Inferences
- Top brainrot hits must sit far above these anecdotes. Treat ≥10% D1 and ≥8 min sessions as a **minimum viability floor**, not a hit benchmark.

### Gaps
- No Naavik/GameRefinery/Deconstructor of Fun retention numbers were accessible for these games. RDC 2025/2026 talks with retention data were not found.

## Q4: UEFN feasibility and Epic-rule sensitivity per lever (brief; a separate researcher covers the rules in depth)

### Takeaway
Epic now allows in-island transactions, including **paid random items**, but only with odds shown before purchase. Paid random items are blocked in several countries and 18+ only in the UK and Brazil. Prize wheels tied to transactions and pushy purchase prompts toward minors are prohibited. Core free brainrot levers (offline progress, theft, events, rarity) are not restricted by any rule I can cite. Paid RNG and urgency monetization are the high-risk areas.

### Cited Rules
- **In-island transactions / paid random items** were allowed from Jan 2026. Numerical odds must be disclosed before purchase — [Tubefilter 2026-01-09](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/09/fortnites-new-developer-made-item-sales-loot-boxes-yes-outfits-and-emotes-no/), [Epic docs: IIT restrictions](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/in-island-transactions-restrictions-in-fortnite).
- Paid random items are **blocked in Singapore, Qatar, Australia, the Netherlands and Belgium**, and **18+ only in the UK and Brazil** (from Mar 2026) — [Epic IIT restrictions](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/in-island-transactions-restrictions-in-fortnite), [gamingamigos](https://www.gamingamigos.com/post/rules-paid-random-items-fortnite).
- **Prize wheels**: transactions must not directly or indirectly affect prize wheels. Also prohibited: transactions that give nothing in exchange for V-Bucks — [Epic Developer Rules change log](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules-change-log), [UE forum announcement](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/update-to-fortnite-developer-rules-in-island-transactions-and-prize-wheels/2692650), [Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/epic-games-bans-prize-wheel-and-loot-box-sales-in-fortnite-creator-islands/).
- **Minors and pressure**: purchase language must not be misleading or overtly pressuring. Developers must not pressure or directly prompt a minor to buy, or to ask a parent to buy. Direct prompts such as "Buy now!" require `RestrictDirectPromptsToPurchase` — [Fortnite Developer Rules](https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules).
- No explicit rule on countdown timers or limited-time offers was found in the snippets (see Gaps).
