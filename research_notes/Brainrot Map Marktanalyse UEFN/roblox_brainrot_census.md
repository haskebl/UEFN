## 1. Census: which brainrot games exist, and what are their numbers?

### Takeaway
I identified 47 Roblox brainrot titles (46 in the table plus one listed in section 3). Hard numbers are very skewed:
- **Collect-and-bring-back-to-base "steal" tycoons dominate.** Steal a Brainrot peaked at about 25.8M CCU, and its offspring Escape Tsunami For Brainrots peaked at about 5.0M CCU.
- **Plants vs Brainrots (garden/tower defense hybrid)** was the only other game to break 5M CCU.
- **Every other title sits in the 10K–700K peak-CCU range, or tens to hundreds of millions of visits.**
- **Per-game revenue:** credible figures exist only for Steal a Brainrot and Escape Tsunami.

## 3. What is rising now (summer–autumn 2026) vs. what peaked in 2025? Is the trend rising, plateauing or fading?

### Takeaway
**The brainrot trend on Roblox is fading from its extreme peak and settling into a persistent, high-churn sub-genre. It is plateauing, not dead.**

**Peak phase:**
- **2025 (May–Oct):** Steal a Brainrot, Plants vs Brainrots, Brainrot Evolution. This was the all-time peak.
- **Jan 2026:** Escape Tsunami For Brainrots gave a second, smaller peak at about 5M.

**Since spring 2026:**
- Launches reach only 10K–90K CCU and fade within weeks: Swing Obby, Sail, Fall, Throw Lucky Blocks.
- In August 2026 the top of Roblox was held by non-brainrot titles (Murder Mystery 2, Animal Hospital, +1 Speed Keyboard Escape, Brookhaven).
- Steal a Brainrot itself is at about 113–215K CCU.

**Still rising:**
- New variants such as Steal A Brainrot Egg (about 13.6M visits in 5 weeks since 2026-08-23).
- Fortnite/UEFN clones.

### Inferences
- **Status: fading or plateauing.** The breakout ceiling for a new brainrot game fell from about 25M (2025) to about 5M (Jan 2026) to below 100K (spring–autumn 2026). The half-life of new titles is now weeks.
- **The base is not zero.** The #1 title still holds about 100–200K CCU and adds about 2B visits per month.
- **What is rising in summer–autumn 2026:** new traversal or hazard-verb variants of the steal-tycoon (egg-steal, lucky-block throw), party/social formats (Split or Steal), and "+1 Speed"-style incremental skins.
- **Legal risk:**
  - Spyder Games actively sues clones: at least 4 suits, including one against a Fortnite map.
  - Ownership of the characters themselves (Tung Tung Tung Sahur and others) is disputed.
  - Any UEFN brainrot map should avoid copying Steal a Brainrot's specific art, objects and layout, and should avoid characters whose ownership is contested.
